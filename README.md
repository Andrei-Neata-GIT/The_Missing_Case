# The Missing Case

**A First-Person Psychological Horror Experience | Made with Unity**

## About the Game

**The Missing Case** is a story-driven first-person horror game developed in Unity as a university project within one month.

The game combines exploration, survival horror, cinematic storytelling, and environmental interactions to create an immersive and unsettling experience.

Players take on the role of **Tom**, a curious young man determined to investigate a mysterious case involving people who have entered an abandoned location and never returned.

Despite being warned by his friend Robert, Tom decides to investigate the location himself, unaware of the horrors waiting inside.

**Some secrets are better left buried.**

## Story

It all begins with a conversation between two friends, Tom and Robert.

After hearing about a mysterious location where several people have disappeared, Tom becomes interested in investigating the case.

Robert warns him that going there is a terrible idea. Many people have already tried searching the area, but none have returned.

Instead of becoming frightened, Tom grows even more determined to uncover the truth.

Before Tom leaves, Robert wishes him good luck.

What begins as a simple investigation soon turns into a terrifying journey filled with disturbing discoveries, dangerous enemies, and secrets that should never have been uncovered.

## Game Features

- **First-Person Horror Gameplay** – Explore immersive and atmospheric environments.
- **Story-Driven Experience** – Follow a narrative with a beginning, cinematic sequences, dialogue, and an ending.
- **Enemy AI** – Encounter dangerous enemies throughout the game.
- **Combat System** – Defend yourself using weapons and limited ammunition.
- **Inventory System** – Collect, manage, and use items during exploration.
- **Interactive Environments** – Interact with doors, elevators, objects, and other elements.
- **Flashlight System** – Navigate dark environments using a flashlight.
- **Camera System** – Record and investigate unsettling environments.
- **Cinematic Cutscenes** – Experience scripted sequences that develop the story.
- **Dialogue System** – Follow conversations between characters.
- **Save System** – Save and continue game progress.
- **Main Menu** – Navigate the game's interface and settings.
- **Character Animations** – Animations for characters, enemies, and gameplay interactions.
- **Voice Acting** – Original voice performance for Tom, the main protagonist.
- **Atmospheric Horror** – Dark environments, unsettling lighting, and suspenseful encounters.

## Development

**Engine:** Unity  
**Language:** C#  
**Genre:** First-Person Horror / Survival Horror  
**Platform:** PC  
**Development Time:** 1 Month  
**Project Type:** University Game Development Project

The Missing Case was developed within a limited timeframe, with the goal of creating a complete horror experience rather than a short technical demonstration.

The project focused on combining gameplay mechanics, cinematic storytelling, character interactions, and environmental design into one playable game.

Some character models and animations were sourced from **Mixamo**, while additional visual assets and development resources were used to accelerate production.

My main focus was implementing, integrating, customizing, and refining the gameplay systems, story sequences, interactions, and overall player experience.

## Development Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was completing an entire game within just one month.

I originally planned to develop the project using Unreal Engine, but because I was still unfamiliar with the engine, I decided to use Unity instead.

This allowed me to focus more on gameplay implementation, programming, enemy behavior, animations, storytelling, and creating a complete experience.

Another challenge was combining multiple gameplay systems while maintaining a consistent horror atmosphere.

## Project Goal

The main goal of The Missing Case was to create a complete, playable horror experience with a clear storyline, interactive environments, intelligent enemies, cinematic scenes, and a satisfying conclusion.

The project also helped me develop my skills in Unity, C# scripting, game design, AI behavior, animation integration, level design, and game development workflows.
